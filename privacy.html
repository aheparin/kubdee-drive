Privacy Policy

Kubdee Drive App — last updated: 18 November 2026

Who uses this app

This app is a personal tool used only by its developer, with the developer's own Google account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data the app accesses

After the owner signs in with Google, the app uses the Google Drive permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This permission only allows the app to see and manage the files and folders that the app itself created in the owner's Google Drive. The app uploads, downloads and organizes the owner's own video files and related metadata in those folders.

How data is stored

Sign-in credentials (OAuth tokens) are stored encrypted on the owner's own computer. The app does not send Google user data to any server operated by the developer or to any third party. There is no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared, transferred or disclosed to anyone.

Retention and deletion

Data stays in the owner's Google Drive and on the owner's computer until the owner deletes it. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete the stored credentials by disconnecting the account in the app.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
