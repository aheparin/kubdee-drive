Kubdee Drive App

Kubdee Drive App is a personal desktop tool that lets its owner back up and organize their own video files in the owner's own Google Drive account.

It is not a public service. It is used only by its developer, with the developer's own Google account.

The app asks for one Google permission, drive.file, which lets it access only the files and folders that the app itself creates in Google Drive.
